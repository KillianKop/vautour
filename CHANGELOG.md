## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-24

First public version. Reads flight packages version 1 from cheminee 0.1.0.

### Added

- Route choice screen with weather summary and data credits.
- Heading-up moving map on the relief, lift overlay, route and turnpoints, overview mode, pan and pinch zoom.
- Manoeuvre banner (climb target, glide distance and heading, speed to fly, arrival margin), next step, vario with
  sound, ground speed, ETA.
- Voice prompts in French and English.
- Hazard reports (9 humorous kinds, 3 variants each), alerts ahead of the pilot with votes, demo pilots.
- Flight simulator (×30 to ×300), end-of-flight summary.
- French interface by default, English option remembered between sessions.
- Phone support: home-screen manifest and icons, safe areas, audio unlock on first tap, screen kept awake in
  flight.
- Unit tests (Vitest), end-to-end tests on Android and iPhone profiles (Playwright), CI and GitHub Pages
  deployment.
